AOIPulse

Privacy Policy · Publisher: Aoitex · Last updated: 2026-08-04

AOIPulse is a puzzle game with offline modes and optional online features. We collect as little data as possible.

What we store on your device

  • High score and local settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, skin, display name)
  • Online profile id / display name cache (for reconnecting to rooms and leaderboards)

These values stay in app storage on your device.

Optional online features

If you use Online Race, Daily Challenge sync, or Leaderboards, the app connects to our game server and may send:

  • Display name you choose
  • Room join / ready / match results (score, lines, time, stage)
  • Leaderboard scores for the mode you submit

We do not require a Google account login inside the game. Online play uses a generated player id stored on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics / crash trackers in the current store build
  • No contacts, location, microphone, or camera data
  • No sale of personal data

Permissions

  • Internet — optional online rooms, daily seed / leaderboards, and server health checks
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings)

Core offline modes work without using online features.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children beyond what is needed for optional online play (display name + scores).

Contact

For privacy questions, email the support address listed on the Google Play store listing for AOIPulse.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the store listing.
