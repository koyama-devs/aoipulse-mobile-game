AOIPulse

Privacy Policy · Publisher: Aoitex · Last updated: 2026-07-31

AOIPulse is an offline puzzle game. We designed it to collect as little data as possible.

What we store on your device

  • High score
  • Settings (sound, music, vibration)

These values stay on your device only. They are not uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or login
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics / crash trackers in the current build
  • No internet permission required for core gameplay
  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, location, microphone, camera)

Permissions

  • Vibration (optional, can be turned off in Settings)

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Aoitex through your store listing support email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the store listing.
