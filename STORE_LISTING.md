## Full description (EN)

AOIPulse is a modern falling-blocks puzzle from Aoitex.

Build stacks, clear lines, and push your high score in a smooth, offline game made for one-handed mobile play.

**Features**
- Classic 10×20 board with fair 7-bag piece randomizer
- Ghost piece, hold, soft drop & hard drop
- Combo scoring and rising difficulty
- Touch gestures + on-screen controls
- Sound, music, and vibration settings
- Local high-score save — no account required

Perfect for short breaks or long high-score runs. No ads in the core offline experience.

Made by Aoitex.
