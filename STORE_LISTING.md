## Full description (EN)

AOIPulse is a modern falling-blocks puzzle from Aoitex.

Build stacks, clear lines, and push your high score in a smooth game made for one-handed mobile play. Play fully offline, or jump into online rooms and daily challenges when you want competition.

**Features**
- Classic, Adventure, Sprint (40 lines), Ultra (2:00), Daily Challenge, No-rotate
- Online public lobby + host approve join, or join by room code
- Shared-seed races, rankings, and adventure attacks (garbage / banana / bomb)
- Ghost piece, hold, soft drop & hard drop
- Combo scoring, themes, and block skins
- Touch gestures + on-screen controls
- Sound, music, and vibration settings
- Local high-score save — no account required for offline play

Made by Aoitex.
